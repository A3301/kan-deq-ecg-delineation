Exploratory mechanism contrast, specified before running this supplementary experiment.
Question: is loss of P onset after replacing the most P-selective decoder channel
specific to the KAN head when the GELU control receives the identical intervention?
Use LUDB v1.0.1 lead II only, fixed 41 held-out subjects and each model's original
disjoint validation fold. Twenty existing weights: 2 heads x 2 runs x 5 folds.
Both heads sit on the same encoder/decoder design but all weights were trained separately.
Within each validation fold rank the 8 layer-normalised input channels by mean in P
minus the maximum class mean among QRS, T and labelled background. Select the largest.
The energy-matched comparator is the other channel closest in mean squared raw activity.
Replace one raw channel across the whole window by its validation-only mean, then
apply the original head and preprocessing. No calibration on the internal test set.
Score P-onset F1 in percent at the original LUDB tolerance (38 samples at 250 Hz),
within original label spans with the same greedy matching, control, taper and windows.
Compute pooled event F1 separately per fold on the same 41 subjects, then average
the 5 F1 values within a run. Report selected minus intact and selected minus
energy-control for each head and run; the paired KAN-minus-GELU difference of the
latter contrasts is secondary. Percentile intervals resample subjects across all
models, runs, folds and conditions together (n=10000). Inference is conditional
on the two fitted runs. This is a perturbation of internal activations.
