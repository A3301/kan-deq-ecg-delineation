No hyperparameters or weights may be changed here.

Use complete PhysioNet LUDB 1.0.1, 200 participants, 12 simultaneous 10-second leads, each independently delineated in source annotation files (reader identities are unavailable). Use the six frozen RDB-trained fits, seeds 1,2,3 for each of the KAN and GELU heads. Original 500-to-250 Hz resampling, zero-phase 0.5–40 Hz, median/IQR per lead, overlapping tapered 2,048-sample windows, four-class segmentation and the original run merging/minimum lengths. No cross-lead information during model inference. Decode original WFDB files, retain native milliseconds. Record leads with identical annotation payloads, partial QRS and edge annotations. Verify model/subject split hashes, compare original lead-II 41-participant event-count CSV at exact counting precision. The new study has no additional clinical adjudication.

For lead II, score all six landmarks at 150/40 ms using the same original greedy nearest-distance one-to-one matching on annotated events. Unmatched output is an FP only in the lead's first-to-last labelled sample interval. Separately report the full 200 and disjoint 159/41 groups. Compute six pooled F1 scores, their unweighted arithmetic mean per seed, then average across three fixed runs within each head. CI on F1 and KAN-GELU difference uses joint resampling of participants, 10,000 bootstrap replicates with RNG seed 20261125. Use no invented boundaries.

QRS durations: reference complexes must have an onset, labelled QRS peak and offset at non-edge native samples with positive elapsed duration. Retain count of incomplete/excluded events. Predicted complexes similarly cannot touch acquisition edges and require the original QRS run length (>=10 samples with gaps <5 bridged). Evaluate per lead inside that lead's first-to-last annotated time interval based on the complex midpoint. If a lead has no usable reference coverage, report separately rather than fabricate negative coverage. Duration is elapsed offset-onset. Match reference and predicted complexes on positive interval overlap and absolute midpoint difference <=150 ms; sort by midpoint distance then reference and prediction order; no restriction on error of either boundary or duration. All in-span unmatched reference and unmatched predicted complexes remain in denominators.

Primary duration endpoint for both the pooled 12-lead annotations and for the independently assembled 12-lead complexes: proportion of ALL eligible reference complexes detected with absolute duration error <=20 ms. 20 ms is a descriptive engineering tolerance, not a clinical pass-fail criterion. Sensitivity tolerances 10 and 30 ms. Report total eligible references, total predictions, matched, missed, and extra; sensitivity, positive predictive value, successful-duration precision (correct/ALL predictions), successful-duration F1, conventional matched duration bias and MAE. Report the original both-heads/all-six-fits common-reference conditional MAE and its coverage alongside. Provide each lead separately and per participant counts across the 12 leads. Main quantity pools labelled complexes within each fitted seed and then averages the three percentages/MAEs; participant bootstrap keeps all leads, seeds and model predictions together. Individual lead intervals and subgroup/threshold results are descriptive, without multiplicity claims.

Operational global QRS: in each participant group annotated intervals by their native midpoints using independent per-lead interval sets (predicted intervals grouped without access to references). Process all intervals by midpoint, then lead index and onset. Assign to the closest current group median among compatible groups with no repeated lead and a new maximum pairwise midpoint separation <=120 ms; if none, start a new group. This deterministic span avoids long chains and prevents duplicate use of a lead. Measure elapsed time from earliest onset to latest offset among the grouped leads, consistent with the multilead measurement principle in Kligfield et al., JACC 2007, doi 10.1016/j.jacc.2007.01.024. Primary assembly requires all 12 leads observed for that beat on each side. Sensitivity assembly requires at least 8 distinct leads (support can differ between model and reference). Score resulting intervals with same overlap/midpoint rule, requiring their midpoint in intersection of the 12 annotation spans for equal observable follow-up; for records lacking a common span or one or more leads, primary global QRS is unavailable, counts must be reported. Report number of eligible participant records, partial reference and partial predicted groups, spread in endpoints, and strict-12 coverage. The strict denominator cannot represent beats lacking complete multi-lead references; compare >=8 explicitly. The reference is assembled from simultaneous lead-specific marks and is not a new clinician-adjudicated global QRS duration.
