Derivation of a multilead QRS interval from cardiologist-marked lead-specific boundaries. Example selected by record ID (LUDB 1) and temporal proximity to the middle of the recording among all-12-lead reference complexes. A, simultaneous lead waveforms scaled independently for display; teal marks the annotated QRS region. B, native individual-lead onset and offset times. Orange dashed lines delimit the earliest onset at 5234 ms and latest offset at 5346 ms (elapsed width 112 ms). Time zero is the midpoint of the resulting multilead interval. This panel illustrates the measurement rule and contains no model predictions. Source: Kalyakulina et al., Lobachevsky University Electrocardiography Database v1.0.1, PhysioNet, doi:10.13026/eegm-h675.
