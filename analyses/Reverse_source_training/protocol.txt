Exploratory reverse training-source experiment, 25 November 2026

Question: after retraining entirely on RDB v4 lead II, do a 404-parameter spline KAN head and a 397-parameter GELU head still yield similar six-boundary delineation on the original 41 held-out LUDB subjects, lead II? This reverses the earlier experiment. It changes both the training source and the number of training records, and uses single models instead of five-fold ensembles. Previous LUDB results from other weights are known to the investigators. There will be no validation, hyperparameter selection, threshold choice or selection of the reported training seeds based on LUDB.

The RDB v4 archive and source map will be verified against SHA256 d24b8c98635bd14c358b58fc432d82db6eb830b8597cb4793e50f43fe3df04d7 and 5ad900a244a0171f0b49a5e4f402311ac5d521f8564c994f696f66a64bcf7f9a. Use all 2,398 previously deduplicated source recordings. Using their sorted record IDs, independently shuffle within the eight source-mapped rhythm groups using generator seed 20261125, assign the nearest integer to 20% in each group to validation and the remainder to training. The released source-record identifiers do not identify every unique patient. Use the same split, preprocessing and minibatch schedules for both heads. No RDB test partition is needed. LUDB original held-out test set is the sole reverse external test.

For 500-Hz lead-II signals, use the existing 250-Hz bandpass and median/IQR transformation. Map valid closed RDB wave intervals to included 250-Hz sample centres (ceil[start/2], floor[end/2]). Remove duplicate intervals and reject empty, inverted or out-of-bounds rows. Preserve visible within-wave samples when an interval touches the recording edge, but do not create a boundary at the edge for event scoring. Outside the earliest/latest valid interval the loss ignores the signal. Within that span, annotated P/QRS/T intervals are classes 1/2/3; uncovered samples are background, including in records without a P annotation. Overlapping intervals of incompatible classes are ignored. This is supervision conditional on reference completeness. Do not impute P waves or manufacture edge boundaries. Summarize invalid, conflicting, ignored, and absent-P counts.

Reuse the exact published UNetKAN encoder/decoder design, with either a head-only KAN or GELU MLP of width 29. No spline bottleneck, other pretrained weights or augmentation. Use seeds 1, 2 and 3. Within each seed initialize the shared backbone identically and use identical uniform recording/crop draws. Train both heads for exactly 2,400 updates each, batch size 32, random crops of 1,024 samples, cross-entropy averaged over valid samples, AdamW with fixed learning rate 0.001 and weight decay 0.01. No scheduler, no hyperparameter search. Every 400 updates compute masked validation cross-entropy on the RDB validation recordings with the same tapered two-window probability average as for test inference. Retain the lowest validation loss checkpoint for each run, resolving ties toward the earlier update. The training loop may be resumed mechanically from a complete run without changing these choices. Run all six models before opening LUDB predictions. The chosen three seeds and source-level evaluation avoid selection by test-set performance.

On the 41 original LUDB test participants, score lead II, six waveform boundaries, one-to-one greedy smallest-distance matching, unmatched predictions counted within the first-to-last annotation interval. Use the originally published WFDB marks at the native 500-Hz timestamps; predicted times on the 250-Hz grid. Compute exact ±150 and ±40 ms tolerances. Include individually observed onsets or offsets even if the other side is missing. Aggregate TP/FN/FP across participants for each boundary and take the unweighted mean over the six F1 values per seed, then the mean over seeds. Compare KAN minus GELU (percentage points), bootstrapping the same 41 participants across all heads and seeds for 10,000 draws (RNG seed 20261125). ±150 ms is the primary reverse endpoint; ±40 ms, individual seeds and landmarks are secondary. Report all outcomes and intervals conditional on the fitted seeds. This is a retrospective research extension, not a pre-registered clinical result or formal equivalence test.
