Objective: determine how the Stage-1 eight-architecture LUDB comparison changes over three fixed training seeds. Use the original 200-participant LUDB v1.0.1, its 41 locked test participants and the five pre-existing development folds. Keep all 12 leads together in the participant partition. Use the archived winning learning rate and default architecture of each model. The original archive contains all five folds of seed 1 for every architecture and seed 2 for U-Net, U-Net-KAN, DEQ and unrolled DEQ. All new fits use the same 40 epochs x 60 updates, batch size 32, span-constrained 1024 crops, original per-crop waveform augmentation, four-class cross-entropy (ignore -1), AdamW with original weight decay, OneCycleLR, gradient clipping at 1, and validation-only checkpoint selection by cleaned 150 ms six-boundary mean F1 at epochs 5, 10, ..., 40. 'Weighted half-width' here refers only to span-constrained crop placement (identical to archived source); no loss or example weighting. New fits run on one GPU rather than on the archived CPU and use independently generated, paired seed-specific crop schedules; comparisons are conditional on this mixed-runtime design. All original models are strictly loaded and re-evaluated under one inference/scoring implementation.

Primary completion target is 8 models x 3 seeds x 5 folds, i.e. 24 five-fold probability ensembles (120 fold fits, 60 originally available, 60 newly trained). Generate missing jobs in a fixed order, by fold, then seed; fold 0 is the prespecified secondary same-fold/same-test panel. If a result must be reported before all jobs, only a complete fold panel can be reported and must be labelled as such; never conflate one fold with a five-model ensemble. Do not choose folds/seeds on performance. For the DEQ compute inference on the two windows of one 10-s lead at a time; implicit stopping must not combine unrelated leads. For the other networks evaluation-only batching of separate windows is algebraically factorized. Export original/new weight hashes, selected epoch and validation F1 for new fits, all six pooled landmark counts, per-participant counts and seed-specific ensemble F1 (150 and 40 ms, as in the original). Pair subject-cluster bootstrap resampling (10,000 draws) across each fixed seed and model, with the percentage-point F1 contrast averaged across the three fitted seeds. Summarise the observed between-seed SD/range descriptively, not as 3 x 41 independent patients. Restore proportions and units as reported.
