Exploratory single-lead QRS duration audit in RDB v4. Written after observing earlier boundary-F1 results but before inspecting any QRS-duration results. No model training, retuning or patient management claims. This analysis concerns time between the onset and the offset of the SAME QRS interval on fixed lead II. PR and QT are outside scope.

Retain the same 2,398 source recordings with measured native lead-II boundaries. Read type 1 (QRS) start/end from each original *.ii.txt, preserve native fractional 500-Hz indices. Use elapsed duration 2*(offset-onset) ms. Discard malformed, repeated identical, reversed/zero-length or out-of-range intervals and intervals touching either recording edge. Index each retained interval by its published annotation-row position within its source ECG. On the model 250-Hz grid, derive class-2 runs from the frozen masks with the original gap merging (<5 samples) and minimum length (10 samples). Use 4*(offset-onset) ms and exclude predicted runs touching either recording edge. Both reference and prediction centers must lie in the previous per-record evaluation span stored in cohort.csv. Include all eligible reference and predicted intervals in the reported coverage account.

Match within source recording. A pair must intersect for a positive duration and have center distance at most 150 ms. Greedy closest-center one-to-one matching, stable by reference row and prediction onset. No duration-error cutoff or condition on individual boundary error. Store reference, prediction, coverage, precision and matched-event duration errors separately for every model and seed. The overlap and midpoint rules can still exclude extreme failures.

Evaluate two named contrasts: KAN head versus matched GELU head, and original DEQ versus eight-step twin. For each contrast, primary duration analysis uses the intersection of the native reference IDs matched by all four outputs (two models x two fitted training seeds) so models and seeds are compared on exactly the same QRS complexes. Report four-way common-reference coverage and seed-specific two-model intersection counts. Per seed, average absolute and signed duration errors over matched QRS complexes, pooling events; bias is predicted minus annotated milliseconds. Average the two fitted-seed estimates for each arm, then compare the first arm minus the second. A negative MAE difference favors the first. Quantify uncertainty by 2,000 paired bootstrap draws of whole source records stratified by the eight original rhythm groups and conditional on the two seeds (random seed 20261125). Supply rhythm counts and descriptive estimates without subgroup significance claims. Multiple beats from a given record always travel together. Interpret the conditional estimates alongside coverage and reference-absence errors; do not infer global twelve-lead QRS duration, diagnosis or clinical thresholds. The two contrasts can have different common-reference sets.
